# Terasilicon IQ — Engineering Website

Official website for **Terasilicon IQ**, a semiconductor engineering services company providing specialized ASIC/SoC engineering support across the RTL-to-GDSII lifecycle.

## About

Terasilicon IQ provides engineering support for semiconductor companies across:

- Formal Verification
- RTL & Design Verification
- STA & Timing Closure
- EMIR & Power Integrity
- Physical Design & Signoff
- Physical Verification
- ASIC/SoC Implementation
- VLSI Engineering Training

The website is designed to present the company's engineering capabilities, solutions, services, and contact information through a clean, professional semiconductor-focused interface.
