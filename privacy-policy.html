Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Terasilicon IQ Private Limited ("Terasilicon IQ", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with this website.

The website does not currently provide user accounts or a website-hosted enquiry form. If you contact us through an email link, we may receive the information contained in your message, which can include your name, email address, company, project information, recruitment information, and other information you choose to provide.

Our hosting infrastructure may also process technical information generated when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security-related logs.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to engineering, business, recruitment, support, or other enquiries.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, or record-keeping requirements.

3. Legal basis and applicable data-protection law

Where data-protection law applies, we process personal data only for permitted purposes and on an applicable lawful basis. For users in India, this includes compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the applicable rules and commencement provisions in force from time to time. We will provide notices and honor applicable rights and consent requirements to the extent required by law.

4. Third-party services

The site currently requests IBM Plex Mono and Space Grotesk font resources from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google to retrieve those resources. The website also links to external services including LinkedIn, Google Maps, and email services. Once you leave this website, the external provider's own terms, privacy practices, and cookies apply.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The current site does not intentionally set first-party analytics, advertising, or personalization cookies through its own JavaScript, and it does not currently use a third-party analytics platform. See the Cookie Policy for more detail.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website or communications, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law. Enquiry information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, legitimate business records, dispute handling, security, or legal obligations.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your rights and privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, updating, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or other controls over your personal data. You may also contact us with a privacy question or complaint. To exercise an applicable right or raise a grievance, email hr@terasiliconiq.com with enough information for us to understand and respond to the request.

9. Children

This website is intended for business and professional audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through this website.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the website, services, or applicable legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date above identifies the current version published on this site.
