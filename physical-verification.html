ENGINEERING APPROACH

What we provide

Formal verification uses mathematical proof to exhaustively analyze RTL behavior against intended properties and design intent. It is especially useful for finding corner-case failures that simulation may miss and for building high confidence before signoff.

  • Assertion-based property verification for control, protocol and safety behavior.
  • Sequential and combinational equivalence checking for RTL, revised RTL and implementation changes.
  • Counterexample-driven debug to isolate the failing state, sequence and root cause.
  • Assumption and constraint development to model legal operating environments without hiding real bugs.
  • Proof convergence support using abstraction, decomposition, coverage and proof-depth analysis.

WHERE IT FITS
