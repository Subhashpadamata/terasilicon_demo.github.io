ENGINEERING APPROACH

What we provide

Physical design turns the logical implementation into a manufacturable layout through floorplanning, placement, clock-tree construction, routing and optimization. Signoff requires coordinated closure across timing, power, physical rules and implementation quality.

  • Floorplanning and implementation planning.
  • Placement optimization and congestion analysis.
  • Clock Tree Synthesis and clock-quality analysis.
  • Routing, optimization and physical violation investigation.
  • Physical closure support across timing, congestion and implementation constraints.

WHERE IT FITS

From requirement to signoff

Our engineering support is structured around the actual closure problem: understand the requirement, isolate risk, apply the right analysis or verification method, and provide clear evidence for the next signoff decision.

Engagement can be focused on a specific block, flow, violation class or closure milestone, while keeping communication aligned with the wider semiconductor team.
