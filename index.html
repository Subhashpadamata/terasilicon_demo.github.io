FROM TALENT TO ENGINEERING TEAM

We don't build resumes. We build engineering teams.

Individual engineers are developed within a common engineering environment, evaluated through sustained technical work, and progressively organized into teams based on project requirements. A project doesn't need one person with one skill — it needs people who can work together.

How engineers are engaged: engineers are developed within the TSIQ ecosystem through structured engineering work, technical evaluation and continuous performance assessment — not a classroom, and not a placement pipeline.

This is engineering problems, internal development and project-connected technical work — kept tied to the real work of a semiconductor engineering company, rather than every engineer sitting on live customer deliverables at all times.

WHY CONTINUOUS EVALUATION MATTERS

Engineering performance becomes the signal.

A short assessment can't show how an engineer behaves under sustained engineering conditions. A sustained development period — typically on the order of six months — provides enough time to observe technical growth, debugging ability, discipline, communication, consistency and ownership.

BUILDING PROJECT-READY CAPABILITY

Readiness depends on the actual project.

Not every engineer is automatically project-ready after a development cycle — different projects need different technologies, nodes, tools and experience levels. The objective is to continuously build strong capability that can then be evaluated against specific requirements: what the project needs, which disciplines it touches, which engineers have demonstrated relevant capability, what project-specific preparation remains, and who can be assigned — on an evidence-based basis, not a resume.

Technologies Nodes Tools Methodologies Experience levels Design complexity Customer requirements
Project requirement → engineering capability
What does the project require? Scope the actual technical demand.
What technical capabilities are needed? Break the requirement into engineering disciplines.
Which engineers have demonstrated relevant capability? Match against observed performance, not resumes.
What additional project-specific preparation is required? Close any remaining gap before deployment.
Which engineers can be assigned? Form the team with a clear, evidence-based basis.
