ENGINEERING APPROACH

What we provide

Static Timing Analysis evaluates whether the design meets timing requirements across operating modes and corners. Timing closure combines constraints, path analysis and implementation feedback to systematically remove setup and hold violations.

  • SDC constraint development, review and consistency checks.
  • MCMM analysis across required modes, corners and operating conditions.
  • Setup and hold analysis with critical-path investigation.
  • Timing violation classification and root-cause analysis.
  • Closure support through implementation and optimization iterations.

WHERE IT FITS

From requirement to signoff

Our engineering support is structured around the actual closure problem: understand the requirement, isolate risk, apply the right analysis or verification method, and provide clear evidence for the next signoff decision.

Engagement can be focused on a specific block, flow, violation class or closure milestone, while keeping communication aligned with the wider semiconductor team.
